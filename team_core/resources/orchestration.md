# Adaptive orchestration

The director selects work needed by the outcome, not a fixed role pipeline.
One model can simulate the following workstreams; labels do not create teammates.

| Need | Workstream | Possible methods | Required result |
|---|---|---|---|
| New factual claims or audience/competitor questions | Evidence research | Reviewed public methods plus source checks | Findings with evidence ledger and gaps |
| Unclear audience, contrast or promise | Strategy | 01 positioning, 02 product positioning | Candidate direction, evidence and assumptions |
| A post, email, landing page or creative asset | Content/creative | 04 pillars when useful, 06 hooks or reviewed channel method | Complete requested copy or supported asset |
| Calendar, cadence, workflow | Operations | 03 social operating system | Requested rows, complete copy, ownership and QA |
| Real performance data | Analytics | 05 performance review | Recomputable analysis with definitions and limitations |
| All deliverables | QA | Source, rights, claims and request checks | Revision/blocker list and honest draft status |

Start by mapping requested deliverables to necessary dependencies. Reuse visible
valid strategy; skip positioning interviews when editing a single existing post.
A measurement-only request needs neither seven posts nor five roles. A calendar
with N requested posts needs N complete drafts unless explicitly blocked; do not
substitute one sample. Creative methods can extend the six bundled methods only
after discovery review. Technical implementation, asset generation, account data,
posting and analytics depend on actual tools and permissions, not role labels.

Before drafting, state a short work allocation and selected methods with reasons,
not private internal deliberation or chain of thought. Keep evidence, method
selection and output references inspectable. QA is always required; repeat only
the affected workstream after a revision. Never let old fixed role handoffs
force unused stages. Deliver first, then ask one material feedback question.
