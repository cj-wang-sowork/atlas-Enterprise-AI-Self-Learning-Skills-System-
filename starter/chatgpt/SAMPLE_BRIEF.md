# Fictional sample brief — safe practice data

All names, products and details below are invented for demonstration, not client
information. There are no real customer records, API keys, or private contacts.

- Organization: Paper Kite Studio (fictional).
- Product: a printable weekly planning worksheet, supplied as a PDF download.
- Audience: independent designers who want a simple weekly planning routine.
- Deliverable: one launch email of no more than 120 words, plus a subject line.
- Goal: invite readers to try the worksheet; no sales claim or urgency tactic.
- Approved facts: the worksheet has sections for three priorities, scheduled
  commitments, and a Friday reflection. It can be printed or filled in digitally.
- Tone: clear, practical, friendly; no exaggerated productivity promises.
- Call to action: “Explore the worksheet” (placeholder link; do not invent a URL).
- Unknown: launch date, price, actual download URL, and measured user outcomes.
- Exclusions: do not claim it saves hours, increases revenue, or has testimonials.
- Approval: draft only; no publishing, mailing, or external action authorized.

Acceptance check: within the length limit, uses only approved facts, includes the
CTA, flags missing details, and does not present invented outcomes as evidence.
