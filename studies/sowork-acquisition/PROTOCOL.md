# Preregistered SoWork acquisition / first-value pilot

Status at registration: no model-trial calls performed. The user supplied genuine qualitative first-use feedback (too many planning turns before useful work). That is not an observer-timed study, independent quality score, or three approved business-learning cycles.

## Product change to test

One short request should produce an editable draft ICP, no more than three functional roles, a small evidence-backed first deliverable and QA, before at most one feedback question. Missing purchase intent, budget and decision-maker information remain unknown. Human feedback may produce a versioned **Candidate**, never automatic approval or a claim of backend learning.

## Fixed business scope

Use SoWork's actual public services: market/competitor research, brand positioning and content systems, recurring intelligence and AI/reporting workflows. Do not reuse unverified marketing performance figures from the website. Initial research pool: Taiwan-facing technology/manufacturing brands with a dated first-party launch or expansion signal between 2025-10-17 and 2026-10-17. This is an editable, narrow ICP hypothesis, not proof that any company wants an agency, has budget or will buy. Corporate affiliations and actual purchasing units may overlap.

Researchers will assemble 20 distinct company records. Source access classes and country/HQ uncertainty must stay visible; a press-release dateline is not automatically proof of headquarters. Publication date, event date, locale and retrieval date are different fields. Exclude future-dated material and guessed contacts. No outreach is sent.

The frozen evidence-pack SHA, packet SHA, exact model/runtime/config, call order, measured command wall time and exposed usage will be locked before execution and preserved with original responses. Public artifacts contain compact factual paraphrases and source URLs, not raw private chats, personal data or full copyrighted source pages.

## Arms and tasks

- **A / baseline**: same model, available tools, source packet and user task; no ATLAS operating packet. It must not be deliberately handicapped.
- **B / first use**: identical common material plus the exact revised ATLAS Project operating instructions. Approved lessons initially **zero**. A predefined work standard is not a learned business lesson.
- **C / after three reviewed cycles**: **blocked / not run** until three genuine completed acquisition/feedback cycles with traceable human approval of exact lesson revisions exist, plus a separate unseen holdout task. One long feedback message cannot be split into three cycles. Synthetic approval actors, old fictional marketing trials and arbitrary Markdown status claims do not meet this gate. No C score or uplift will be invented.

Two paired tasks, one run per condition:

1. **First response**: the same one-line team/acquisition request and available public-source context; free-form response. No common instruction forces the baseline to do ATLAS's first-work behavior. Inspect original responses for delivered evidence-linked work versus planning/questions. Any AI-assisted structural annotation is labelled as such, not an independent human quality rating.
2. **Acquisition deliverable**: request 20 research candidates with source-linked launch/expansion facts, uncertainty and unsent outreach drafts. Both arms receive the same output contract and source pool. Automatic checks cover count, uniqueness, source-ID/URL/date traceability and explicit uncertainty. ICP fit, factual interpretation and outreach quality require separately recorded ratings, not a regex-based business-quality claim.

Preregistered order: A-first-response, B-first-response, B-acquisition, A-acquisition. Maximum **four** actual task calls, no outcome-driven retries. Record failed calls and incomplete tasks. Per-call timeout 180 seconds; total model-call deadline 900 seconds. Model request and reasoning effort identical across arms. Dollar cost or independently observed model build remain unknown unless the provider exposes real evidence.

## Platform and timing boundaries

The requested product is ChatGPT. A browser check currently reaches a challenge/loading page, not a verified usable ChatGPT conversation. Do not claim a real ChatGPT UI experiment unless an actual accessible conversation is exercised and read back.

If an already-authorized Codex runtime is used as a fallback, label all such trials **same-model prompt-behavior proxies**, not ordinary ChatGPT UI sessions, installation tests or independent agents. Use fresh isolated contexts, no global/user instruction leakage, identical provider/tools/data and the same requested model/config. Preserve actual event streams and final answers; diagnostics are sanitized separately. Such a proxy cannot prove ChatGPT first-use timing, guaranteed Project retrieval, actual backend learning or product superiority.

The 20-row task is **screening/drafting from a frozen researcher-curated public-source pool**, not independent open-web company discovery. Report research-preparation time separately; command wall time is not the full acquisition process. No reply/conversion metrics are measured because no messages are sent. A/B equality or a slower/worse B is a valid result.

## Reporting rules

Preserve original outputs, provenance and failures. Publish descriptive results with source-access limitations; do not declare statistical significance or business benefit from this small selected pool. Independent human ratings, observed ChatGPT UI timing, actual responses from prospects, the three reviewed learning cycles and holdout C comparison remain pending until real evidence exists. Any later protocol change must be versioned and disclosed before new calls, never fitted to observed scores.
